Core Criteria Checklist Summaries

Review these before testing yourself.

  • Physics: Heat transfers via conduction, convection, radiation. Static vs Current electricity. Series vs Parallel. Light = transverse/vacuum, Sound = longitudinal/needs particles. f = v ÷ λ.
  • Reproduction: Asexual (binary fission, runners) = identical, fast. Sexual = gametes, fertilisation, 50% genetics, high variation.
  • Chemistry: Atomic no = protons. Mass = protons + neutrons. Valence electrons dictate reactivity. Metals lose e- (cations), non-metals gain (anions). Equations MUST balance (conservation of mass). Endo (absorbs heat), Exo (releases heat).
  • Carbon Cycle: Continual movement through spheres. Photosynthesis (uses CO2) & Respiration (releases CO2) are linked. Combustion of fossil fuels disrupts balance.
  • Body Reg: CNS (brain/spinal) & PNS. Neurons (dendrite, axon). Endocrine uses hormones in blood (slower, long-lasting). Homeostasis = internal balance (negative feedback).
