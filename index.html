Study Material & Command Words

Command Words Guide: Identify means tell what it is; Describe means outline what it looks like/does; Explain means tell how it works and why it matters; Evaluate means assess its value and importance[cite: 1].

1. Physics — Energy Transfer[cite: 1]

  • Heat: Energy transferred from warmer to cooler objects due to temp difference. Measured in Joules (J). Moves via conduction (solids), convection (fluids), and radiation (waves). Plastics are poor conductors (insulators)[cite: 1].
  • Electricity: Static (stationary charge) vs Current (moving charge). Know how to draw series and parallel circuits, placing ammeters (in series) and voltmeters (in parallel)[cite: 1].
  • Sound & Light: Law of reflection: angle of incidence = angle of reflection. Light travels through a vacuum; sound cannot (it needs particles and travels fastest in solids). Longitudinal waves have compressions/rarefactions. Higher frequency = higher pitch; greater amplitude = louder sound[cite: 1].
  • Formula: f = v / λ (Frequency = velocity divided by wavelength)[cite: 1].

2. Biological Science — Reproduction[cite: 1]

  • Asexual: One parent, identical offspring. Uses binary fission, budding, spore formation, and vegetative propagation. Common in non-flowering plants, protists, bacteria[cite: 1].
  • Sexual: Two gametes (sex cells) join (fertilisation) creating offspring with half genetic info from each parent. Creates greater variation but is slower. Can be internal or external based on environment[cite: 1].

3. Chemical Science — Atoms & Reactions[cite: 1]

  • Atoms: Basic unit of matter. Protons (+), Neutrons (neutral), Electrons (-). Atomic number = protons. Mass number = protons + neutrons. Neutral atoms have equal protons and electrons[cite: 1].
  • Periodic Table: Arranged by atomic number. Rows = periods, Columns = groups (similar chemical properties). Group number helps predict valence electrons. Shells hold 2, 8, 8 electrons[cite: 1].
  • Ions & Equations: Metals lose electrons (cations +), non-metals gain (anions -). Balance ionic charges to write neutral formulas. Chemical equations must be balanced to show conservation of mass (atoms are rearranged, not destroyed). Endothermic absorbs energy, exothermic releases it[cite: 1].

4. Earth Science — The Carbon Cycle[cite: 1]

  • Carbon moves through the atmosphere, hydrosphere, lithosphere, and biosphere. Photosynthesis (carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen) and Cellular Respiration (glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water) are linked processes[cite: 1].
  • Dead organisms decompose or lithify. Combustion of fossil fuels and agriculture return massive amounts of carbon to the atmosphere, interacting across Earth's spheres[cite: 1].

5. Biological Science — Body Regulation[cite: 1]

  • Nervous System: CNS (brain/spinal cord) and PNS. Neurons transmit messages. Receptors detect stimuli. Stimulus-response pathway[cite: 1].
  • Endocrine System: Slower, uses hormones transported via blood to target cells. Maintains homeostasis (e.g., negative feedback for body temperature and water volume)[cite: 1].
