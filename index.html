Organized Revision Modules

Select a subcategory to review all criteria points[cite: 1].

  • Heat: Energy transferred from warmer to cooler object. Measured in Joules (J). Conduction (solids), Convection (fluids), Radiation (waves/vacuum). Plastics are poor conductors (insulators).
  • Electricity: Static (buildup) vs Current (flow). Ammeter placed in series; Voltmeter in parallel.
  • Waves: Law of reflection: angle of incidence = angle of reflection. Light travels in vacuum; sound cannot. Longitudinal waves have compressions/rarefactions. Sound travels fastest in solids.
  • Wave Formula: $f = \frac{v}{\lambda}$ (Frequency = velocity ÷ wavelength). Higher frequency = higher pitch. Greater amplitude = louder sound.
  • Asexual: One parent, identical offspring. Strategies: binary fission, budding, spore formation, vegetative propagation (runners/bulbs). Common in non-flowering plants, protists, bacteria.
  • Sexual: Two gametes join (fertilisation) creating offspring with half genetic info from each parent. Internal or external fertilisation depends on habitat. Creates variation but is slower.
  • Atoms: Protons (+), Neutrons (0), Electrons (-). Atomic number = protons. Mass number = protons + neutrons. Neutral atoms have equal protons and electrons.
  • Periodic Table: Arranged by atomic number. Rows = periods, Columns = groups. Group number predicts valence electrons. First 20 elements config (e.g. Ca = 2,8,8,2).
  • Ions: Metals lose electrons (cations +); Non-metals gain (anions -). Balance charges for neutral ionic formulas.
  • Reactions: Reactants → Products. Must be balanced (change coefficients) to show Conservation of Mass. Endothermic (absorbs energy), Exothermic (releases energy).
  • Carbon continually moves through atmosphere, hydrosphere, lithosphere, and biosphere.
  • Photosynthesis: carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen (using light energy).
  • Respiration: glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water (releasing energy).
  • Dead organisms return carbon via lithification or decomposition. Human combustion of fossil fuels increases CO2 in the atmosphere.
  • Nervous System: CNS (brain/spinal cord) + PNS. Neurons transmit electrical info. Pathway: Stimulus → Receptor → Control Centre → Effector → Response.
  • Endocrine System: Glands produce hormones transported in blood to specific target cells. Slower, longer-lasting than nervous system.
  • Homeostasis: Maintaining a stable internal environment (e.g., negative feedback loops for body temperature or water volume).
